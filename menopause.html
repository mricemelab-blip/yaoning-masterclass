课程概述

Evidence-Based Exercise Prescription for the Menopausal Transition

This course covers evidence-based exercise prescription for perimenopausal and postmenopausal women, including:

  • Hormonal changes during menopause and their impact on training
  • MHT (Menopause Hormone Therapy) and exercise safety
  • Resistance training protocols for bone density preservation
  • Cardiovascular health and aerobic training guidelines
  • Mental health, sleep quality, and mind-body interventions
  • Nutritional strategies for the menopausal transition
